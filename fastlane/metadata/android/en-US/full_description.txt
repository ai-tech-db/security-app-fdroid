Security APP is a modern, privacy-focused local VPN firewall for Android.
Unlike commercial VPNs, it does not route your data to a remote server.
It creates a local VPN tunnel directly on your device to intercept and filter traffic.

Features:
- Local VPN Firewall (DNS + TLS SNI filtering)
- Custom Blacklist
- Suspicious Apps Monitor
- Recent Connections log with configurable retention
- Lookalike Domain Shield
